The 8.5-inch OLED iPad mini heads toward November 6 availability

Apple’s update combines an OLED display and A20 Pro. For a compact tablet, reflections and workspace matter alongside brightness.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-05
Source announcement 2026-10-26

A display update for the small iPad

Apple announced the 8.5-inch OLED iPad mini with A20 Pro on October 26. Its US announcement lists October 28 preorders and November 6 availability. Local order timing and actual shipment depend on the market and configuration.

Contrast, brightness and reflections

OLED pixels emit their own light. Apple says peak brightness exceeds twice that of the preceding iPad mini. Peak output is not a complete measure of readability: reflections, automatic brightness and content affect it too. A nano-texture option changes the surface treatment, a distinct consideration.

Compare tasks, not just dimensions

A compact tablet can suit reading while holding it or checking documents on the move. Working with a full PDF page and notes or placing two apps side by side may favor a larger display. Our analysis is that the right size depends on how often the device is handheld versus used as a divided workspace.

Mobility and remaining checks

The N1 chip supports Wi-Fi 7, whose usefulness also depends on the network. IP67 reflects specified test conditions. Check local configurations, service terms and delivery dates. Independent comparisons are needed for sustained battery life and visibility with the reader’s own content.

Compare with a familiar document

A small tablet is easier to carry, but that does not settle whether a full textbook page and annotations fit comfortably. Compare actual documents and split-screen space. Bright surroundings and on-the-move use also change the importance of reflections and connectivity.
