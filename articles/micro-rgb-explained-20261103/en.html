Micro RGB TV: reading the light source behind the screen

Samsung’s new explainer separates a large-screen experience into illumination, image processing and surface behavior.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-04
Source announcement 2026-11-03

What happened on November 3?

Samsung posted a video explaining its Micro RGB TV technology. This is a technical presentation, not a universal retail launch notice. Local prices, sizes and availability need to be checked separately.

Understanding RGB illumination

RGB refers to red, green and blue. It helps to distinguish the part that produces light from the process that turns it into an image. Samsung highlights small RGB LEDs. That provides a starting point for understanding its color claims, but a product name alone does not establish equivalence with other display architectures.

More than one quality metric

Our analysis is that reflections in a bright room, dark-scene behavior, viewing distance and source material all influence the experience. Image processing and surface reflection control address different problems. One promoted feature cannot demonstrate superiority for every kind of content.

What to compare

Compare displays using similar material and settings, and consider lighting and seating distance at home. This is an explanation of a manufacturer presentation, not an independent measurement or hands-on review. Model-specific specifications, local pricing and warranty remain necessary before a purchase decision.
