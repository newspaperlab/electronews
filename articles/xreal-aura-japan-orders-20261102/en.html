XREAL AURA’s Japan order window opens for wired Android XR

Google announced AURA on October 28 and listed November 2 for Japanese orders. Tethering and app behavior shape practical use.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-05
Source announcement 2026-10-28

Orders opened on different dates

Google introduced the wired Android XR glasses on October 28. It listed immediate orders in the US, Canada and Korea and November 2 for Japan. An order window is distinct from delivery timing, which depends on the local seller and inventory.

What the glasses format changes

XREAL makes glasses-style displays. Android XR puts digital windows into the user’s field of view. A wired design also means arranging the cable and connected hardware. That trade-off matters differently at a desk and on the move.

App compatibility is not one question

Google discusses ordinary Android apps and apps designed for XR. Displaying an existing app window is different from supporting spatial interactions or an input method adapted to glasses. Check the services and actions you actually need rather than relying on the size of an app catalog.

Mirroring or running locally

Wired mirroring can display the source device’s screen, but it requires compatible output and a connection. Running an app on the XR platform brings its own account and control requirements. A travel workspace and a home theater place different demands on those options.

Where practical tests help

The editorial significance is a new format for Android XR. Text clarity, extended comfort, vision correction and behavior in bright surroundings remain matters to assess in the intended setting.

Screen size is not reading comfort

Video and prolonged document work place different demands on sharpness, window placement and movement. A user with several devices at a desk should evaluate where the cable and attached hardware actually sit, alongside the weight of the glasses.
