ThinkCentre X Ultra's November window: read the configuration

The compact PC was announced in September, with up to 128GB of unified memory.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-06
Source published 2026-09-03

Lenovo's September 3 announcement scheduled ThinkCentre X Ultra availability from November 2026, with an expected starting price of €3,100. This is a look at that planned window, not a new launch announcement today.

One shared resource

The 1.6-litre system advertises up to 128GB unified memory. Graphics allocations should not be added again as if they were separate physical capacity.

Capacity helps determine what fits, while processor, cooling and software affect execution. A small enclosure is not itself evidence of sustained-load behavior.

Also distinguish the maximum configuration from the starting price. Check the exact memory, storage, operating system and local availability of a purchasable model.

The practical comparison includes ventilation, cable access and expansion as well as desk space. Choose around the workload rather than automatically buying the maximum specification.
