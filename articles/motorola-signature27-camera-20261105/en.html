Motorola Signature 27 scores 173: a camera ranking needs its boundaries

The manufacturer’s claim is scoped to tested 2026 releases; the test also reports limitations.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-06
Source announced 2026-11-05

Motorola said on November 5 that the Signature 27 earned a DXOMARK camera score of 173 and a Gold Label. Its “best” claim concerns tested smartphones launched in 2026. The DXOMARK test page checked the same day showed second place in the overall ranking. A launch-year subset and an all-time tested-device ranking answer different questions.

A score belongs to a test system

Motorola makes communications devices; Signature 27 emphasizes imaging. DXOMARK measures device quality using its own methods. A combined score summarizes conditions, rather than expressing a physical unit of picture quality. A points difference cannot be converted into a percentage improvement, or extended to every untested phone worldwide.

Specifications include 50MP main and ultrawide cameras and a 200MP periscope telephoto. Periscope optics fold the light path to fit a telephoto assembly inside a thin device. DXOMARK highlights the 3x camera and detail around 3x–6x, but notes weaker very-long-range detail than rivals with dedicated longer-range hardware. More pixels do not ensure equal results at every zoom setting.

Conditions behind the strengths

The test praises exposure, color, autofocus and portraits, while identifying noise with motion in low-light video and reduced detail in extremely dark moving scenes. A stationary night subject differs from a moving child or pet. Separating stillness from movement and photos from video is more useful than a blanket night-camera claim. This is an interpretation of published tests, not our own shooting experience.

Motorola also describes collaboration with Qualcomm and DXOMARK in development and optimization. The evaluator's role and the manufacturer's collaboration disclosure should be read together. Collaboration alone does not establish an incorrect score; it reinforces the value of checking the original strengths, weaknesses, scope and comparisons. Results for a reader's typical scenes can matter more than one total rank.

This is an evaluation announcement, not confirmation of Korean pricing or launch. Software changes may affect processing and future tested devices may change rankings. Record the date, tested population and scene conditions alongside 173. A long-range photography user and someone filming movement in darkness can reasonably draw different conclusions from the same score. Translate the superlative into questions about the images you actually need.
