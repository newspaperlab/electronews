Galaxy Buds On arrives in the US

Samsung Mobile Press announced the US launch of Galaxy Buds On, highlighting an open-ear listening experience.

Newspaper Lab · 2026-10-28 · 4 min
한국어 English 日本語 简体中文

What is open-ear listening?

Open-ear earbuds focus on listening to audio while remaining aware of nearby sounds. Their purpose differs from a sealed design built for immersion. The first question is therefore not how much noise they block, but how often you need to hear the world around you.

What Samsung announced

On October 28, Samsung announced US availability for Galaxy Buds On. Its release describes a clip-on design and a black model scheduled for early November at $229. The announcement date is not the sales date, and converting the dollar price does not establish an official local price.

Who might find the format useful?

Someone walking with a companion or responding to nearby conversations may value an open design. Someone seeking isolation should compare a different type of earbud. These are ways to think about the use case, not conclusions from a hands-on test of this product.

Check the conditions behind the numbers

Samsung lists up to 9.5 hours of music playback, or 38 hours including the case. Those are manufacturer test figures, not guaranteed everyday results. Fit, sound leakage, phone compatibility and local warranty deserve attention before a purchase. Software features may differ by connected device and country.

The broader question

The interesting shift is from asking only how much sound an earbud blocks to considering how audio fits into a shared environment. Product quality still requires independent testing and local-market information.
