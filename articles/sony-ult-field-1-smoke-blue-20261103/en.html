Sony adds Smoke Blue to the ULT FIELD 1

The portable speaker gains a colour option. Its battery and water resistance figures come with test conditions.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-04
Source announced 2026-11-03

A colour update

Sony Europe announced Smoke Blue for its ULT FIELD 1 on November 3. It joins four existing colours. The speaker’s ULT button emphasises bass.

The 12-hour figure

Sony lists up to 12 hours with ULT enabled and volume at 25. Playback, volume and temperature can change that result. Outdoor users should plan charging around their own conditions.

Reading IP67

The first digit covers dust and the second refers to a defined immersion test. Sony tested the speaker at one metre for 30 minutes with the cap secured. Impact damage may affect protection. Local prices and availability need a regional check.
