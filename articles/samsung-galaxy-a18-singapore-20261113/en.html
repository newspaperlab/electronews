Galaxy A18 goes on sale in Singapore for S$328

The LTE configuration has 4 GB of memory and 128 GB of storage, with up to six OS generations of upgrades.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-14
Source announced 2026-11-13

Samsung announced and began selling the Galaxy A18 in Singapore on November 13. The 4 GB/128 GB LTE version costs S$328 in light blue, black or silver. The release covers Singapore and does not establish a Korean configuration, date or price.

The Galaxy A line includes Samsung’s entry and midrange phones. The A18 uses a 6.7-inch FHD+ Super AMOLED display and Helio G99+ processor. AMOLED controls light at pixel level. The diagonal and resolution class cannot establish outdoor brightness or sustained game frame rates.

Samsung specifies LPDDR5. The 4 GB memory serves running apps, while 128 GB storage holds apps and files. RAM Plus uses some storage as virtual memory. It can help with switching between apps, but does not add physical memory. Consider how much space remains for photos and apps.

The rear cameras are 50 MP wide, 5 MP ultrawide and 2 MP macro. Each provides different framing. Their pixel counts do not add to a single image resolution. The 5000 mAh figure measures battery capacity, and 25 W describes the promotional adapter. Actual runtime and charging duration also depend on usage and conditions.

Samsung promises up to six OS generations and six years of security updates. Major versions and the duration of security support are separate measures. Regional timing, app and language support for search and voice features need checking. We see memory and storage usage as practical considerations when keeping the phone over that support period.

LTE identifies the published 4G configuration. A similarly named phone elsewhere may have different connectivity. With an import, check the model number, carrier bands and activation terms. Regional repair and parts policies can also differ, so the listed price is only one consideration.

Selected Singapore channels list a S$30 discount through December 8 and selected stores offer a 25 W adapter without a cable, subject to channel and stock. Compare the normal price with the offer’s actual contents and terms.
