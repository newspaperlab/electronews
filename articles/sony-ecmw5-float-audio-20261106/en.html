Sony ECM-W5: what 32-bit float can and cannot simplify

The November European sales plan brings recording flexibility, with compatibility conditions.

Newspaper Lab · Published 2026-11-06
Source published 2026-10-14

Sony announced the ECM-W5 on October 14, planning European availability in November at €410 or £350 for the set. Compatible cameras can record 32-bit float audio with video.

Start at the microphone

Float recording offers more room for level adjustment in post-production. It does not automatically repair wind, clothing friction or a microphone positioned too far from the speaker.

Transmitter backup and monitoring complement wireless transmission. Understand where the backup is stored and how it will be aligned with the picture afterward.

MI Shoe functionality and USB-C connections have their own device requirements. Check the exact camera and desired recording mode, not just connector shape.

A short test recording and listening check remain valuable. Reliability comes from the workflow and equipment together rather than the file format alone.
